# Product and variant naming register

Reviewed 19 November 2026 using a fresh read-only snapshot of the configured catalog database. Every one of the 160 products and 397 variants is included in the accompanying workbook. All names below are proposals; no live names, SKUs, URLs or statuses have been changed.

## Naming rules

Use **protection designation + equipment family + stable construction/model qualifier** for the product. Use **verified configuration attributes** for its variants. Display the complete selection as **Product — Variant**.

- Keep the existing `FLP/WP` and `Non-FLP` distinctions. A naming exercise does not verify certification or justify adding a protective designation to an unmarked product.
- Use consistent words: Bulkhead, Floodlight, Streetlight, Tube Light, Well Glass, Cleanroom, Indicator, Control Station, Enclosure, Replacement LED Driver and Replacement LED Module.
- Use `45 W`, `230 V`, `16 A`, `7.5 HP`, `19 mm`, `18 in`, `3-pin` and `2-entry`. Preserve decimals, fractions, exact voltages and thread standards. `2 × 30 W` means two lamps and must not become `30 W` or an assumed single 60 W lamp.
- Keep shared housing footprints or model references in the product name during the first cleanup. Put changing depth, lamp power, entry count and fitted options in variants. Full dimensions must have confirmed units and axis order.
- Use Enclosure for empty housings. Keep complete fittings, enclosure-only supply, kits and individual replacement parts distinct.
- Retain existing valid SKUs and type numbers. A display-name change does not require changing either. Keep old names as searchable aliases.
- Replace vague Big, Small, Mini, Standard, Unit, Old Model and Copy wording with a verified model or defining characteristic. Large Body, Compact Body and the legacy trade qualifiers in this register are interim proposals requiring the noted checks.
- Product-level material, protection and certification statements must apply to all variants. Mixed brass/stainless or ET/NPT families require technical review before regrouping.
- Parenthetical `to Confirm` text is an explicit review placeholder, not a final sales name. Complete the missing information before publishing. Patterns list desired attributes; unrecorded values have not been invented.
- Approval fields in the workbook are editable and start as Pending. Repeated suggested product names represent possible future common families, not an approved merge or a direction to reactivate archived items.

## Apply the register

1. Review active products first in the Products sheet. Approve the spelling and family wording, then complete any missing technical identity. Fill Approved name only with the final wording you want.
2. Review its matching rows in Variants. Preserve every physical difference, resolve rating/name contradictions and fill Approved variant. A suggested label is not approval to alter its saved specification.
3. Protect issued document snapshots before editing live product names or specifications. The previous review found live-data dependencies in quotation and work-order copies.
4. Apply approved display changes against the stable product and variant IDs. Preserve SKUs, type numbers, serial numbers and historical links. Do not derive an update from row position or product name.
5. Handle consolidation separately. Compare construction, materials, certificate scope, components and existing usage before selecting a retained record and creating an equivalence mapping. Keep archived records archived unless separately reviewed.
6. Add family-specific fields and generate future names from approved attributes. Show matching existing configurations before users request a new product or variant.

The workbook has two filterable sheets: Products contains all 160 product proposals and variant patterns. Variants contains all 397 old-to-new variant label proposals, existing SKUs, type numbers, saved ratings and stable record IDs.
